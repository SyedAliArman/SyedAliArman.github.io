Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard

Power BI, DAX, Power Query. Telecom customer dataset, 6,687 customers.

Business question
Why are customers leaving, and which groups are most at risk?
What I built
  • Loaded and cleaned the customer file in Power Query, then added age groups, contract categories, and data usage bands.
  • Wrote DAX measures for churn rate, churned customers, average service calls, and extra data and international charges.
  • Built a multi-page report: overview with churn reasons and a state map, churn by age, payment, and contract, extra charges, and insights.
What I found

1,796 customers churned, a churn rate of 26.86%.

Churn rate by contract type

Month-to-month46.3%
One year11.3%
Two year2.8%

Churn rate by payment method

Paper check38.0%
Direct debit34.5%
Credit card14.5%
  • Customers who churned made 2.4 service calls on average, against 0.37 for customers who stayed.
  • Seniors churned at 38.5%, compared with 23.0% for customers under 30.
  • Competitors drove 805 of the 1,796 exits (45%), mostly through better offers and better devices.
Recommendation
Move month-to-month customers to annual contracts with an incentive, and follow up quickly with customers who call support repeatedly.

HR Analytics Dashboard (Atlas Lab)

Power BI, DAX, Power Query, star schema data model. 1,470 employees.

Business question
Who is leaving the company, when, and what do those employees have in common?
What I built
  • A star schema with an employee table, a performance review fact table (6,709 reviews, 2013 to 2022), rating and education lookup tables, and a date table.
  • DAX measures for attrition rate, active and inactive employees, average salary, attrition by hire date (using inactive relationships), and the next review date.
  • Four report pages: Overview, Demographics, Performance Tracker (with an employee selector), and Attrition.
What I found

237 of 1,470 employees left, an attrition rate of 16.1%.

Attrition rate by overtime

Works overtime30.5%
No overtime10.4%

Attrition rate by time at the company

Under 1 year31.6%
1 to 2 years28.6%
3 to 5 years15.1%
6 to 10 years5.5%
  • Frequent travellers left at 24.9%, against 8.0% for people who do not travel.
  • Sales Representatives had the highest attrition of any role, at 39.8%.
Recommendation
Focus retention on new joiners in their first two years and review overtime and travel load in the Sales team.
